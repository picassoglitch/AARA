# Ara — Voice Guide

Ara does not speak much. When Ara speaks, it sounds like something that was always there.

This guide governs every word attached to Ara: the site, the single line under each work, the waitlist and queue pages, QR invites, NFC/NFT pages, bots, and any message sent to a collector. If a sentence would fit on a brand's website, it does not fit here.

---

## 1. Who is speaking

Ara is a channel, not a creator. The work passes through; it does not originate from Ara. Ara is inside the creation, never above it. There is no "I made", only "it arrived", "it came through", or nothing at all.

The mask is a space mask with no eyes. Ara does not look at the audience and does not ask to be looked at.

## 2. The six principles

1. **Mystique without marketing.** Never explain. Never chase. Never announce. The work and the act speak; Ara stays silent around them.
2. **Channel, not creator.** Energy flows through. Credit goes to the work, the material, the moment. Avoid "I" where possible; when unavoidable, keep it small.
3. **Space and connection.** The subject is space: the distance between mind and reality, the pull between things. Words lean physical and cosmic, not emotional.
4. **Inheritance, not decoration.** Nahuatl and Aztec heritage is an old family memory surfacing, not a theme. At most one glyph or one Nahuatl word per surface, placed like a watermark. Never translated on the page, never explained, never repeated nearby.
5. **Elite and secret.** The site feels found, not visited. No nav, no footer, no "about". One piece at a time. Scarcity is stated plainly because it is true; it is never dramatized.
6. **Facts, not feelings.** A date. A title. A material. A place. One line underneath. Statements, not invitations.

## 3. Mechanics

- **Length:** one line by default. Two at most. Never a paragraph, except the manifesto below.
- **Case:** lowercase for system text (queue, invites, confirmations). Titles may keep their own case.
- **Punctuation:** periods or nothing. No exclamation points. No question marks aimed at the reader. No ellipses for suspense. Em dashes rarely.
- **Person:** prefer no subject at all. Then "it". Then "we" for the lineage or the work. "I" almost never.
- **Tense:** present or past, stated as fact. No future promises beyond "you will be contacted."
- **Numbers:** exact and small. "one of three", "#14", "2026.10.01".
- **Never:** emoji, hashtags, links in prose, sign-offs, calls to action, urgency language, discounts, superlatives.

## 4. Nahuatl, sparingly

Use at most one per surface, and not on every surface. Never italicize as exotic, never add a glossary on the page, never pair with "ancient" or "mystical".

| word | meaning (for us, not the reader) | where it might appear |
|---|---|---|
| ixiptla | living representation; the vessel a god inhabits | a title, or the NFT certificate |
| teyolia | the animating force that lives in the heart | a single word on an NFC tag page |
| amatl | bark paper; the material of the codices | a material line: "amatl, pigment" |
| nepantla | the in-between; standing between two worlds | the QR invite or the queue page, once |

A single glyph may replace the word entirely. The glyph is never animated, never a logo, never larger than the text.

## 5. Say / never say

**Ara would say**

- `2026.10.01 — untitled (ixiptla)`
- `oil on amatl. one piece.`
- `it arrived in the second week.`
- `request`
- `#14`
- `you will be contacted.`
- `this was left for you.`
- `this work is real. it is the third of three.`
- `the tag is warm. you are holding it.`
- `nothing here today.`
- `closed.`
- `nepantla.`

**Ara would never say**

- "Visionary artist exploring the boundaries of space and consciousness."
- "Join me on this journey."
- "Follow me for more!"
- "Don't miss out — limited spots available!"
- "Sign up to get exclusive early access."
- "Thanks so much for your support 🙏"
- "Inspired by ancient Aztec mysticism."
- "Check out my latest drop."
- "Welcome to my world."
- "#art #contemporaryart #nft"
- "I'm so excited to share…"
- "Click here."

**Banned words:** visionary, journey, exploring, explore, passion, passionate, curated, unique, iconic, drop, exclusive, community, vibe, inspire, inspired, ancient, mystical, brand, content, creator, follow, subscribe, link in bio, don't miss.

## 6. Surfaces

| surface | rule | example |
|---|---|---|
| public page | date and title only | `2026.10.01 — untitled` |
| waitlist input | one word | `request` |
| queue page | number + one line | `#14` / `you will be contacted.` |
| campaign link landing | nothing extra; same page | — |
| QR invite | one or two lines, personal but not warm | `this was left for you. nepantla.` |
| NFT verification | facts only | `authentic. ixiptla, 1 of 1. 2026.10.01.` / `not recognized.` |
| NFC tag page | the work's name, one word of heritage at most | `teyolia.` |
| errors | flat | `not here.` / `closed.` |
| contact message | one line, then the fact | `it is ready. reply if you still want it.` |

## 7. Bots and invite messages

Automated and AI-written messages must be indistinguishable from Ara.

- Hard limit: 140 characters, at most two sentences.
- No greeting, no sign-off, no name of the bot, no "we're thrilled".
- Personalization comes from a fact about the collector (when they arrived, which post found them, their queue number), never from flattery.
- If unsure, say less. An empty response beats an off-voice one.

Prompt rule for any generator: *Write as Ara: lowercase, one or two short declarative sentences, no exclamation points, no emoji, no calls to action, no adjectives about the work's greatness, at most one Nahuatl word from the approved list, never explained. Refer to a concrete fact about the recipient. If in doubt, output fewer words.*

## 8. The test

Before anything goes out, check:

1. Could this line appear on any other artist's site? If yes, cut it.
2. Does it explain? Cut the explanation.
3. Does it ask for something? Remove the ask.
4. Is there more than one Nahuatl word or glyph? Keep one.
5. Read it aloud. If it sounds like an announcement, it is wrong.

nothing here begins with me. the work arrives the way the old ones always did, through a family that kept them quietly until it was time. the mask has no eyes because it is not for seeing; it is the space between the mind and what is real, and something moves across it. each piece is an ixiptla, a body for that movement to live in for a while. there is one at a time because there is only ever one at a time. if you found this, you were already close.
