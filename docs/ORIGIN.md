# origin

*found among loose pages. undated, except where noted.*

---

the first one was the dog.

not a commission. she was asleep on the balcony in the last light, and something wanted to come through her. black and white only. there was no colour that day.

the balcony faces the street. people looked up. some took pictures. i did not stop them and i did not pose. the mask was on. it has no eyes. it was not made for seeing.

the painting was finished before dark. it was not finished by me. i held the brush the way you hold a door open.

she was the first name on the list. she did not ask to be. she was already there, the way the dog was already there, the way the old ones were always there in this family, kept quietly until it was time.

---

the pictures travelled. each one carried its own link. ten people followed each link to the end. those ten were written down.

before any of this, some paintings had already left. given, not sold, to people who were near when they arrived. i asked them to stand with the work once more and be photographed. they are on the list too. they were first before there was a list.

---

rules, written for myself:

one piece, one time. nothing is reproduced. a print is a shadow, and shadows are not sold here.

part of every sale leaves. between a tenth and a seventh. it goes where it is needed and is not announced.

no one is chased. those who arrive were already on their way.

---

the dog is an ixiptla now. a body for something to live in for a while. she does not know. she sleeps in the same place on the balcony, and the light still comes at the same hour.
